# Phonely Airways Policy Knowledge Base

## Refunds

Refundable fares can be refunded to the original form of payment until 24 hours before departure. Non-refundable fares receive airline credit minus any fare difference. Government taxes are always refundable.

## Changes

Most tickets can be changed once without a service fee. The traveler pays any fare difference. Changes must be completed before scheduled departure.

## Same-Day Changes

Same-day confirmed changes are allowed when another flight has seats in the same cabin. Same-day standby may be available at the airport.

## Baggage

One carry-on and one personal item are included. Checked bags can be added after booking. Fees depend on route and fare class.

## Support Escalation

Customer support should handle accessibility requests, payment issues, complicated multi-city itineraries, unaccompanied minor travel, and any caller who asks for a human representative.
